🎵 Harmoniq Help Guide

How It Works

Harmoniq creates personalized 20-song playlists based on your emotional journey. Select your current emotions, choose where you want to be, and get a 3-phase playlist that guides you through validation, transition, and destination.

Getting Started

  1. Click the Harmoniq icon in your Chrome toolbar
  2. Select up to 3 current emotions
  3. Click Next and select your target emotions
  4. Click Generate Playlist
  5. Save your playlist or export to Spotify

Spotify Integration

Click "Create in Spotify" to copy your song list. TuneMyMusic will open where you can paste and transfer songs to Spotify. Premium users can use direct API creation after setting up credentials.

Cloud Sync

Sign in with Google to save playlists to the cloud and access them across all your devices.

Auto-Fetch Songs

Use the Setup Wizard to automatically fetch 600 songs from Spotify's curated playlists. You'll need a Spotify Developer account (free) to get API credentials.
