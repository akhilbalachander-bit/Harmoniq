How to use Harmoniq

1
Pick your current mood

Select up to 3 emotions. Filter by genre using the chips at the top.

2
Choose your destination

Select where you want your mood to go. This shapes the end of your playlist.

3
Generate your journey

Harmoniq builds 20 songs in 3 phases: where you are, a transition, and where you want to be.

4
Like, dislike, or block

Thumbs up likes a song. Thumbs down skips it next time. Block removes the artist from future playlists.

5
Save and sync

Save locally or sign in with Google to sync across devices.

6
Create in Spotify

Directly creates the playlist in your Spotify account. Falls back to copying the song list if not connected.
