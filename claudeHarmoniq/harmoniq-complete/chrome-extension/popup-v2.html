How to use Harmoniq

1
Pick your current mood

Select up to 3 emotions that describe how you feel. Use the genre chips above to filter by music style.

2
Choose your destination

Select 1–3 emotions you want to feel after listening. Harmoniq builds a path between where you are and where you want to be.

3
Generate your playlist

Tap Generate. A 20-song journey is built in three phases: validation (current mood), transition, and destination.

4
React to songs

Use 👍 to like, 👎 to dislike, or 🚫 to block a song. Preferences are saved and shape future playlists.

5
Save your playlist

Name it and tap Save. It appears in the Playlists tab. Tap 🎵 there to send any saved playlist straight to Spotify.

6
Add to Spotify

Tap the green Spotify button to create the playlist directly in your account. If that fails, the song list is copied to your clipboard.

7
Sync across devices

Sign in (top-right) with Google or email to back up playlists to the cloud and access them anywhere.
