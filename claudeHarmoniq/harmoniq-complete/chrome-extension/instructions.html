Harmoniq Help

Everything you need to get started.

1

Pick your current mood

Select up to 3 emotions that describe how you feel right now. Use the genre chips to filter by music style.

2

Choose where you want to go

Select up to 3 target emotions. This is the mood you want to reach by the end of the playlist.

3

Generate your playlist

Harmoniq builds a 20-song journey in three phases: songs matching where you are now, transition songs, and songs that guide you to your destination.

4

React to songs

Tap the thumbs-up to like a song. Tap thumbs-down to avoid it in future playlists. Tap the block button to stop seeing any songs from that artist.

5

Save your playlist

Give it a name and tap Save. Stored on this device. Sign in with Google to sync across devices.

6

Create in Spotify

Creates the playlist directly in your Spotify account. On first use, you will be asked to connect your Spotify account. Falls back to copying the song list if not connected.

7

My Playlists

Switch to the Playlists tab to see everything you have saved. Tap Show to expand the track list, or Delete to remove it.
