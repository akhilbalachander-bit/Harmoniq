How to use Harmoniq

Your emotional music journey in 7 steps

1. Pick your current mood

Select up to 3 emotions that describe how you feel right now. You can also filter by genre (Pop, Hip-Hop, Rock, etc.) to narrow the music style.

2. Choose your destination

Select 1–3 emotions you want to feel after listening. Harmoniq builds a path between where you are and where you want to be.

3. Generate your playlist

Tap Generate. A 20-song journey is built in three phases: validation (your current mood), transition, then destination.

4. React to songs

Use thumbs-up to like, thumbs-down to dislike, or the block button to hide a song permanently. Preferences are saved and shape future playlists.

5. Save your playlist

Name it and tap Save. Your playlist is stored locally (and in the cloud if signed in) and appears in the Playlists tab.

6. Add to Spotify

Tap the green Spotify button to create the playlist directly in your account. If that fails, the song list is copied to your clipboard instead.

7. Manage saved playlists

Open the Playlists tab to view saved playlists. Expand tracks, click any track to search on Spotify, or tap the Spotify button to push the whole playlist.

Tip: Sign in (top-right corner) with Google or email to sync playlists across devices and back them up to the cloud.
