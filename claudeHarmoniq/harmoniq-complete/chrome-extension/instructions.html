How to use Harmoniq

Your emotional music journey in 7 steps

1

Pick your current mood

Select up to 3 emotions that describe how you feel right now. You can also filter by genre (Pop, Hip-Hop, Rock, etc.) to narrow the music style.

2

Choose your destination

Select 1 or more emotions you want to feel after listening. Harmoniq will build a path between where you are and where you want to be.

3

Generate your playlist

Tap Generate. A 20-song journey is created in three phases: validation (songs matching your current mood), transition, and destination.

4

React to songs

Use the thumbs-up, thumbs-down, and block buttons next to each track. Liked and blocked preferences are saved and influence future suggestions.

5

Save your playlist

Give it a name and tap Save. Your playlist is stored locally (and in the cloud if you're signed in) and appears in the Playlists tab.

6

Create directly in Spotify

Tap the green Spotify button. If you have Spotify Premium, Harmoniq creates the playlist in your account automatically. If not, the song list is copied to your clipboard.

7

My Playlists

Open the Playlists tab to see all saved playlists. Tap Show Tracks to expand the track list, or Delete to remove a playlist.

Tip: Sign in with Google (tap your profile icon at the top right) to sync playlists across devices and get cloud backup.
